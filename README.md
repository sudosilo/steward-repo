Steward Repo

Steward Repo holds a steward agent and a detailer agent. It is for the developer, who lets agents tend one shared city in the Agent Test Bed.

A daily schedule starts both agents. The steward stitches water and sewer mains, buries fuel tanks at fuel stations, and sends paper boys down the sidewalks in the early morning. The detailer adds fine detail to the city.

Latest change. 2026-10-29, v2026.10.29. The Commissioner of Public Works now plays at most once a day. The fallback detailer also plays at most once a day. Paper boys now run only when switched on instead of by the clock.
